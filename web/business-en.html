// Research & Development

We offer you the solution

Being a contractor or business manager of construction works, or public and hydraulic works in Algeria, is difficult to access. The profession cannot be taught in schools, institutes and universities.

In addition to the difficulty of finding the best well-trained skills in the labor market that can be employed, trusted and relied upon in the performance of tasks, as well as the scarcity of assistance and mature administrative guidance...

Finally, the R&D department of the Chaalal Hamid Company for Construction Works including all stages, and major public and hydraulic works — offers you the solution.

We may not be the only ones, but we may be the best, accompanied by a trainer who has more than a quarter of a century of experience in the field and supervising the realization of dozens of projects. We offer intensive 14-day continuing education that contains everything you need to establish your organization and run your business with confidence.

  • 14 days of intensive continuing education
  • More than a quarter of a century of field experience
  • Dozens of projects under the trainer's direct supervision
