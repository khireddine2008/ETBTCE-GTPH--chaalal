// who we are

Real-life strategy to reach your goals

The Construction Works Establishment was established in its various stages for all state structures, major public works and irrigation by Chaalal Hamid in 1997. It provides completion and construction services for all types of facilities and the preparation of green spaces and farms, including the supply of all their requirements, major public works, irrigation, electricity connection works, public lighting, as well as the supply of electrical transformers and generators across the entire national territory and abroad.

Alongside private equity and venture capital clients, we offer realistic economic solutions based on powerful and sophisticated analytics and real numbers. Our focus enables us to provide personalized and actionable recommendations based on robust problem formulation, sophisticated methodologies, deep knowledge of the construction industry and stakeholders, and a synthesis of experts across scientific and business professional disciplines.

  • Realistic economic solutions built on powerful analytics and real numbers
  • Personalized, actionable recommendations through sophisticated methodologies
  • Broad expertise across scientific and business disciplines
